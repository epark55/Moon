Lunar

Times are shown in your local time zone. Phase times for October to December 2026 match published almanacs; other months are calculated and are typically accurate to within an hour or so. Illumination and age are calculated for local noon on each day, and for right now on today's date. Moonrise and moonset are calculated for the coordinates you enter, shown in your device’s time zone; your location stays in this browser. The horizon setting raises the horizon to match trees, hills or buildings in the direction the Moon rises, so moonrise comes a little later and moonset a little earlier. A supermoon here means a full moon closer than about 227,000 miles.

Save to your home screen

iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.

Android: open this page in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen. The lock screen widget below is iPhone only.

iPhone only. Scriptable is not available on Android. Android users can add the web app to the home screen instead (see above).

  1. Install the free Scriptable app from the App Store.
  2. Tap Copy widget code below.
  3. In Scriptable tap +, paste, and name it Lunar. Tap the play button once and allow location.
  4. Add a Scriptable widget to your lock screen or home screen and choose the script Lunar.

When the app is updated, copy and paste the code again to update your widget.






Tip: if anything looks out of date or off, tap Today to reload the app with the latest times, weather and features.


Cloud cover forecasts for the next two weeks come from Open-Meteo.com. Your coordinates are sent to them when you set a location, and also once to OpenStreetMap to look up the name of the nearest town. Nothing else is sent.
