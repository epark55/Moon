Lunar

Times are shown in your local time zone. Phase times for October to December 2026 match published almanacs; other months are calculated and are typically accurate to within an hour or so. Illumination and age are calculated for local noon on each day. Moonrise and moonset are calculated for the coordinates you enter, shown in your device’s time zone; your location stays in this browser. Horizon height raises the horizon to match hills or buildings in the direction the Moon rises (0 is a flat horizon, and about 0.5 to 1 is common in hilly areas). A supermoon here means a full moon closer than about 227,000 miles.
