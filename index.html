Lunar

Times are shown in your local time zone. Phase times for October to December 2026 match published almanacs; other months are calculated and are typically accurate to within an hour or so. Illumination and age are calculated for local noon on each day. Moonrise and moonset are calculated for the coordinates you enter, shown in your device’s time zone; your location stays in this browser. The horizon setting raises the horizon to match trees, hills or buildings in the direction the Moon rises, so moonrise comes a little later and moonset a little earlier. A supermoon here means a full moon closer than about 227,000 miles.

Cloud cover forecasts for the next two weeks come from Open-Meteo.com. Your coordinates are sent to them when you set a location, and nothing else is.
